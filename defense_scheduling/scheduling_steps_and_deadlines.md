## Steps to schedule the defense

1. **Set the date** with all committee members **and the defense chair**.
2. **Submit two forms** to **Ruth and Siyuan** once the date is set:
   - *Dissertation Defense Committee Member and Defense Chair List* form
   - *PhD Degree Certification* form
3. **Submit the dissertation manuscript** to the committee **and** the GCCIS PhD
   Director **≥ 4 weeks before** the defense date.
4. **Submit title, abstract, and Zoom link ≥ 2–3 weeks before** the defense.
   - If in-person or **hybrid**, notify Ruth & Siyuan of date/time **≥ 3 weeks
     before** so a room can be reserved.
5. **Submit a publication list** to the GCCIS PhD Director (cc Ruth & Siyuan),
   **clearly highlighting the one publication** that fulfills the PhD milestone
   requirement.
6. **Apply for graduation** in SIS (separate from the defense — see deadlines).

## Key dates — Summer term 2258

| Date | What |
|------|------|
| **Aug 5, 2026** | Last day to apply online for Graduation (Summer 2258) |
| **Aug 7, 2026** | Grad School *recommended* date to submit paper + signature page to ProQuest (first week of finals) |
| **Aug 14, 2026** | Certification date on diploma |
| **Aug 21, 2026** | Latest recommended ProQuest submission if minor revisions are needed |
| **Aug 26, 2026** | Final certification due to registrar — all degree requirements (incl. dissertation + any minor revisions) must be complete |

> Working backward from an August defense: with the **4-week manuscript rule**, the
> manuscript must reach committee + Director ~4 weeks before the chosen August date,
> and forms/room/abstract logistics need 2–3 weeks of lead time. Pick the defense
> date soon and back-plan from it.

## Commencement / ceremony note (for reference)

The Spring 2026 cycle (Siyuan, 2026-01-22) required defending by **April 30** to
walk in **May 2026** commencement (Doctoral Hooding May 8, 2026). A **summer
(August) defense** means certification in summer; **ceremony participation would be
the following year's commencement**. Regalia/commencement-book deadlines (April 1)
applied to the spring cycle.
